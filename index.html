/ 01 — THE SHOP

Fifteen years of focus,
one workshop floor.

Greg Taylor founded GT Accuracy on a single principle: every rifle leaves the shop tighter than it has any right to be. Two decades of work behind a Clausing, an Acra, three HAAS CNC mills, and a Doosan coming online.

We build for benchrest competitors, F-Class shooters, and serious hunters who can tell the difference between "good enough" and "where the hell did that group come from."
