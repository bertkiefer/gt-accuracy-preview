THE SHOP

FIFTEEN YEARS
OF FOCUS

Greg Taylor founded GT Accuracy on a single principle: every rifle leaves the shop tighter than it has any right to be. Two decades of work behind a Clausing, an Acra, three HAAS CNCs, and a Doosan that's about to come online.

We build for benchrest competitors, F-Class shooters, and serious hunters who can tell the difference between "good enough" and "where the hell did that group come from."

FOUR DISCIPLINES
ONE STANDARD

Whatever the target — paper at 1000 yards, a coyote at 400, or a record book — we build the rifle to do exactly one job, perfectly.
