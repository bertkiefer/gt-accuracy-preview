/ 02 — WHAT WE BUILD

Built for the
discipline you shoot.

Custom rifle builds for Benchrest, F-Class, Predator, Varmint & Big Game Hunting. Whatever the target, we build the rifle to do exactly one job — perfectly.

01 BENCHREST

Shooting in the 1's.

In the pursuit for shooting in the 1's, GT Accuracy is the first choice for serious competitors. Our custom benchrest rifles are always built to the strictest standards for accuracy using world-class methods on Borden BRMXD receivers.

RECOMMENDED ACTION Borden BRMXD
02 F-CLASS

F-Open & F-TR.

F-Class consists of two disciplines: F-TR and F-Open. Rifles in F-Open are limited to 22 pounds, while F-TR is 18 lbs including bipod. We recommend Borden BRMXD or Black Knight XD receivers for F-Open builds, and Borden BRM for F-TR.

F-OPEN ACTIONS Borden BRMXD · BKXD F-TR ACTION Borden BRM
03 HUNTING

Predator, varmint & big game.

There's enough variation that comes into play when you're pursuing big game or predators — why should your rifle be one of them? Build your hunting rifle with the same level of quality as our high-grade competition rifles and hunt with confidence.

DISCIPLINES Predator · Varmint · Big Game
